A basic electric motor: the commutator reverses the loop current every half turn

Animation of a basic electric motor. A rectangular current loop between the N pole of a magnet on the left and the S pole on the right, with the magnetic field drawn as blue arrows from N to S. The loop turns about an axle that recedes into the page. Its near end is wired to a split-ring commutator that turns with it; two fixed brushes connect the ring to a battery. Red arrows show the vertical magnetic forces on the two long sides of the loop, equal and opposite, which turn the loop. Twenty-four snapshots 15 degrees apart show the loop turning; when the ring's gaps pass the brushes the current stops briefly, then flows the other way round the loop, so the forces reverse and the torque keeps the same sense.
