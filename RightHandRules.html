Right-hand rules: cross product, coordinate axes, straight current, current loop

Animation of the right-hand rules. Four right-hand rules shown one after another with a cartoon right hand drawn in three dimensions. Cross product: the fingers line up along vector a and curl toward vector b, and the thumb points along a cross b, out of the page. Coordinate axes: the same with i-hat and j-hat, the thumb along k-hat. Straight current: the thumb points along the current in a vertical wire and the curled fingers follow the circular magnetic field line around it. Current loop: the fingers curl along the current in a horizontal loop and the thumb points along the loop's magnetic field and magnetic moment, upward.
